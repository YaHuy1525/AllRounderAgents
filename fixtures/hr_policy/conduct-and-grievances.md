# Conduct and Grievances

Reviewed: 2026-07-20

Every employee may raise a grievance in writing through the people partner on duty. Grievances are acknowledged within two working days and investigated by a people partner with no prior involvement in the matter.

Interviews during a grievance investigation are confidential. Findings are shared with the employee who raised the grievance and with the people manager only where the outcome affects team duties.

An appeal may be filed within ten working days of the written outcome. Appeals are reviewed by a different people partner, and the appeal decision closes the grievance process.

This handbook is a policy document, not legal advice. Questions about legal entitlements or formal claims are referred to the legal team through the people partner on duty before any commitment is made to an employee.

Disciplinary steps follow the same written record: a documented conversation, a written warning, and a final review. Termination decisions rest with the people partner and the department head together.
