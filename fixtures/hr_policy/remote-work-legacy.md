# Remote Work (Legacy)

Reviewed: 2024-01-10

This 2024 remote-work policy was superseded by the hybrid-work section of the workplace policy. It is kept for reference only and must not be quoted in current answers.

Employees on the legacy arrangement worked remotely up to three days per week. The arrangement was recorded in the employee file and reviewed every six months by the people manager.

The legacy home-office stipend was 500 EUR per employee, paid once with the March payroll. The stipend was replaced by the home-office equipment budget in the benefits policy.
