# Leave and Time Off

Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, prorated for partial years. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year; carried days expire at the end of the following March.

Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 10 calendar days before the first day of leave. The people manager reviews each request against team coverage and the blackout calendar before it is booked into the leave calendar.

Sick leave is tracked separately and does not draw down the annual balance. Absences longer than three consecutive workdays require a medical note uploaded to the employee file by the people partner.

Parental leave provides 20 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 12 weeks at full pay for the secondary caregiver. Parental leave should be requested at least 30 days before the expected start date where practical so coverage can be arranged.

Unpaid leave may be granted for up to 8 weeks per calendar year at the people partner's discretion. Unpaid leave does not accrue annual leave and pauses seniority-based benefits until the employee returns.
