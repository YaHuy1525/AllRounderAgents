# Benefits and Pay

Reviewed: 2026-06-15

Salaries are reviewed once per year in the April cycle. The review compares the role against the published salary bands for its department and level; outcome letters are issued by the people partner before the end of April.

Expense claims are reimbursed when submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date. Receipts are required for any claim above 25 EUR, and travel claims must name the business purpose of the trip.

The company matches pension contributions up to 5 percent of base salary. Employees choose their contribution rate in the benefits portal, and changes take effect from the next payroll month.

Private health coverage is offered to every employee on a permanent contract. Enrollment happens during onboarding or during the annual benefits window in November; later changes require a qualifying life event.

The home-office equipment budget is 800 EUR per employee every two years, covering a desk, chair, monitor, and peripherals. Purchases above the budget need prior approval from the department head and are recorded as company assets.
